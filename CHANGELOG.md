# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-12

- Redirect Fedora's GStreamer video thumbnailer entry to `ffmpegthumbnailer`.
- Raise GNOME's thumbnail cache to 2 GiB with ten-year retention by default.
- Stop stuck thumbnailer processes and restart Nautilus after installation.
- Provide configurable cache limits and a conservative uninstaller.
